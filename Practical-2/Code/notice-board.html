Academic Notices

Official announcements from the academic office and faculty.

Urgent 5 August 2026 Academic Office

Mid-Semester Examination Schedule Released

The mid-semester examination for Semester 3 (Batch 2025–26) will be conducted from 1 September 2026 to 10 September 2026. The detailed timetable with subject-wise dates has been uploaded to the Examination portal. Students are advised to collect their admit cards from the administrative office by 25 August 2026.

Reference No: CEU/EXAM/2026/08/047
Important 3 August 2026 Finance Office

Semester Fee Payment — Last Date Extended

Due to technical issues on the payment portal, the last date for Semester 3 fee payment has been extended to 20 August 2026. Students who have not paid fees will not be permitted to appear in mid-semester examinations. Contact the finance office for queries.

Reference No: CEU/FIN/2026/08/012
General 1 August 2026 Library Administration

Revised Library Timings — August 2026

The central library will remain open Monday to Saturday, 7:30 AM to 9:00 PM during the exam preparation season. Sunday hours: 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM. E-library access via library.charusat.edu.in is available 24/7.

Reference No: CEU/LIB/2026/08/003
Warning 28 July 2026 Dean of Students

Attendance Shortfall Warning — B.Tech Sem 3

Students with attendance below 75% in any subject are advised to meet their class advisor immediately. Attendance below 75% will result in barring from the examination. Detailed lists have been sent to class advisors. Students may apply for condonation with valid medical documentation.
