About StudentHub

We're building the future of academic management — one student at a time.

Our Mission

Why we built StudentHub and who it's for.

StudentHub was born out of a simple frustration: academic information was scattered across dozens of portals, noticeboards, and WhatsApp groups. Students were spending more time searching for information than actually learning.

Our mission is to consolidate every piece of academic information a student needs — attendance, assignments, timetables, materials, and notices — into a single, intuitive platform that works beautifully on any device.

Our Core Values

Principles that guide every design decision and feature we build.

🎯

Student-First Design

Every feature starts with the question: "Does this genuinely help a student learn better?" If not, we don't build it.

🔒

Privacy & Security

Academic data is sensitive. We use strong encryption, minimal data collection, and zero third-party data selling.

⚡

Speed & Reliability

StudentHub loads fast — even on 3G connections. We benchmark every release for Core Web Vitals performance.

♿

Accessibility

We build for everyone — keyboard navigation, screen reader support, and WCAG 2.1 AA compliance are non-negotiable.

🌍

Open & Inclusive

Built for students across all disciplines — engineering, arts, commerce, and science — at any institution.

🔄

Continuous Improvement

We release updates every two weeks based on direct feedback from the student community. Your voice shapes the roadmap.

The StudentHub Story

2023 — The Idea

Started as a semester project at CHARUSAT University. Three friends frustrated by scattered academic portals decided to fix it.

2024 — First Launch

StudentHub v1.0 launched to 200 early-access students. Feedback poured in, and the team shipped 14 updates in the first month.

2025 — Campus Expansion

Partnerships with 10 Gujarat universities brought the user count to 8,000 students. Attendance and timetable modules hit 99.9% uptime.

2026 — Today

Over 12,000 students across 50 campuses rely on StudentHub daily. We're now building AI-powered study recommendations.
